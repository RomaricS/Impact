Programs

From first-time club players to college-bound athletes, there is a program for every level.

01

Club volleyball

Competitive club teams for players ages 12–18. Six teams across Blue and Pink brackets, competing in regional and national tournaments across the Midwest.

02

Recruiting

We actively support college-bound athletes with recruiting resources, film and coach connections.

03

Camps & clinics

Youth development camps and position-specific clinics open to all skill levels throughout the year.

Club fees

Several ways to pay. Always include your player's name and team in the payment note.

  • Fees are due by the first practice of each month.
  • A $25 late fee applies after 5 days past the due date.
  • Checks are payable to Legends Sports Academy.
  • Mail or drop off at 2525 Florida Drive, Fort Wayne, IN 46805.
